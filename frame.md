# Art direction
A jewel-like kinetic sculpture on a midnight studio stage, where a small impulse travels through a tangible machine.
Background #080d19; stage #172332; chrome #e8f4ff; brass #e8b85c; domino sequence coral #ff6854 through golden amber #ffc857 to turquoise #35c8b4 and cobalt #517cff. Color identifies position along the chain.
No typography overlays: the mechanism fills the frame. Focal ball or impact near frame thirds. Rails and repeated dominoes lead the eye. Machined bevels, supporting bolts, polished trim, shadows and environment reflections establish depth. No infographic panels.
Three continuous camera legs: close rolling-ball tracking, low domino follow, spring trigger/gong reveal and restrained retreat.

Supporting material and lighting colors: #34465d, #10151c, #75d7ff, #39a5ff, #b7d6ff, #1e2230, #ffe8c6, #68a9ff, #ffca75, #0c1422, #d7b97f, #f9ab30, #ffc366, #ffc76c. These are physical material and lamp variations within the warm-metal/cool-studio palette.
